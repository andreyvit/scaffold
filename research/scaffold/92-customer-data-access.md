# Customer data access

## Confirmed · C62 · 2026-10-23
Superstaff may access client data. Keep an audit journal of meaningful customer-data access by any user, not only superstaff.

- Opening a customer details page counts as access.
- Merely seeing the customer's name in a list does not count.
- Record bulk work, such as an export, as a bulk operation. Do not enumerate every customer touched by default.

User's reason for the granularity is not separately stated; do not infer a retention or sampling policy. This grants superstaff data access, not all permissions to all staff. Staff/customer identity separation remains deferred. Audit visibility, retention and exact event schema remain unselected.

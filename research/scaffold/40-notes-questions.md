# Questions before notes and Zoo

Already supported: small bounded abstractions; inspectable state; real-flow tests; care for interaction; context-dependent complexity.

Still open:
1. What ordering resolves conflicts between delivery, durability, UX, and ownership?
2. What makes complexity justified before the immediate need arrives?
3. Which engineering role models represent which values? Where do their domains stop being useful?
4. Which project layout rules are deliberate, and why?
5. What belongs in every product: support, metrics, accessibility, recovery, export, audit?
6. Which AI-era notes are original instructions, and which are generated answers or copied quotations?
